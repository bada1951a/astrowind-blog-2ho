## 1. A moisture warning before you need to charge

When a Galaxy Z Fold refuses a USB charger, I would treat the moisture warning as a reason to stop and check the port, rather than rush to bypass it. A nearly empty battery makes the situation frustrating, but resetting software cannot dry a wet connector.

## 2. Separate drying from software troubleshooting

Moisture in the USB connector can block charging. A persistent warning after the port is dry needs further troubleshooting; it does not prove a particular internal component has failed.

## 3. Work through these checks

1. <strong>Dry first.</strong> Switch the phone off and leave it in a dry, ventilated area. Do not force wired charging or insert objects into the port.
2. <strong>Check USB Settings only after drying.</strong> Open Settings → Apps, enable Show system apps using the filter, then find USB Settings → Storage → Clear cache. Names and availability vary by software version.
3. <strong>Escalate a persistent warning.</strong> If a dry device still refuses charging, request model-specific Samsung support. Avoid factory resets or guessed repair prices as a first response.

## 4. Prepare for the next trip

Keep an alternative way to access tickets and essential documents. Use a compatible wireless charger only if your model supports it and the phone's exterior is dry.

## 5. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)



Q1. Does clearing the cache remove moisture?



A1. No. Drying and a software cache check address different possibilities.



Q2. Does this warning prove that the charging board needs replacement?



A2. No. The warning alone is not a component diagnosis.



Q3. Should I change USB debugging to prevent this?



A3. USB debugging is not a moisture-removal step. Keep troubleshooting focused on the connector and the manufacturer's guidance.
